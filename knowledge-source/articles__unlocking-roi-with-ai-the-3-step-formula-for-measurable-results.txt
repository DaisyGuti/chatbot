Riley Stricklin Founder | Chief Strategy Officer Artificial intelligence is reshaping industries, but one question often remains unanswered: What's the ROI? While many organizations are eager to adopt AI, the path to measurable results can feel unclear. The reality is that AI success doesn't come from flashy tools—it comes from strategic implementation. In this post, we'll explore a practical, three-step formula to unlock the ROI of AI, ensuring your investments drive real business outcomes. ‍ Why ROI Is Critical in AI Adoption ‍ For AI to be more than a trend, it must deliver tangible value. Businesses often struggle with this because they focus on technology first, rather than outcomes. Without a clear strategy, AI can quickly become an expensive distraction. A study by Gartner revealed that only 53% of AI projects make it from pilot to production. Why? Because businesses fail to connect AI initiatives to measurable goals, such as increasing revenue, improving efficiency, or reducing costs. ‍ The 3-Step Formula for AI ROI ‍ Achieving ROI from AI doesn't require reinventing the wheel. It starts with focusing on three key steps: ‍ Step 1: Identify High-Impact Areas ‍ Not all problems are created equal. Start by targeting areas where AI can have the most measurable impact. • Look for repetitive tasks that consume time and resources. • Pinpoint bottlenecks in workflows where automation or insights could improve efficiency. • Identify revenue-driving processes that could benefit from optimization. Example: A mid-sized e-commerce business identified its product recommendation engine as a high-impact area. By integrating AI-driven personalization, they boosted average order value by 15%. ‍ Step 2: Start Small and Prove the Concept ‍ The biggest mistake companies make is trying to implement AI everywhere at once. Instead, start with one pilot project to prove its value before scaling. • Choose a manageable, data-rich area for the pilot. • Measure success with clear KPIs, such as reduced costs, increased productivity, or improved customer satisfaction. Example: A logistics company piloted AI in route optimization. The result? A 12% reduction in fuel costs, which built internal momentum for scaling AI across other departments. ‍ Step 3: Measure and Iterate ‍ AI success isn't a one-time event—it's an ongoing process. Once you implement a solution, continuously measure its performance and refine it to maximize value. • Use dashboards to monitor real-time performance metrics. • Gather feedback from teams and stakeholders to identify improvement areas. • Expand into adjacent use cases based on your results. Example: A healthcare provider implemented AI for appointment scheduling and used real-time analytics to refine the system. Over time, they reduced missed appointments by 20%, improving both patient outcomes and operational efficiency. ‍ Key Takeaways for Business Leaders ‍ Unlocking ROI with AI isn't about investing in the most advanced technology; it's about focusing on outcomes that matter. By identifying high-impact areas, starting small, and continuously refining solutions, businesses can transform AI from a cost center into a profit driver.
